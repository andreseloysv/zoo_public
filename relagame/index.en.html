A calm campfire in a forest clearing

RelaGame is a quiet place to look at: a campfire by a stream, tall grass in the wind, a giant mossy tree and cliffs fading into the haze. There is nothing to win and nothing to do — just watch, listen and unwind.

  • Choose the light: dawn, day, dusk or night.
  • Let it rain softly or heavily, or keep it dry.
  • Calm classical and acoustic music, or none — the fire, the stream, insects and frogs keep you company.
  • Drag to turn around the fire, pinch to come closer. Leave it alone and the view drifts slowly by itself.
  • In Spanish, English and German. Works fully offline.
