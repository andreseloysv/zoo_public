Privacy policy

RelaGame for Android and iOS, version 1.0.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In short

  • RelaGame collects no personal data at all.
  • No account, no sign-in, no user identifier.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The game itself never connects to the internet.
  • The developer runs no server on which data about you could be kept.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your choices in a small settings file inside its own private storage on your device: the time of day, the rain level, the music volume and source, the language and the graphics quality. This file never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Purchase and licence check

RelaGame is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies; the developer receives no personal data about you from it, only the store's anonymous sales reports. On Android, Google Play may check that the copy on your device was obtained from Google Play. That check is carried out by Google Play itself, not by the game.

Children

RelaGame collects data from nobody, children included. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising and no in-app purchase.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. Because the developer holds no data about you, an access request can only be answered with: no data about you is held. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever handled data differently, this page would be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top would change. Earlier versions can be traced in the history of this public repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
