Support

How do I change the time of day, the rain or the music?
Tap the round settings button in the bottom-right corner. The panel has the time of day, the rain, the music (play, pause, next, volume) and the language. Your choices are remembered.
How do I move the view?
Drag with one finger to turn around the fire, pinch to come closer or step back. If you leave it alone, the view drifts slowly on its own.
Does it need the internet?
No. Everything ships inside the game, and it works in airplane mode.
My phone gets warm.
The scene is drawn in high quality. Lower the screen brightness, or play while charging for long sessions.
Who made the music and sounds?
All credits and licences are listed in the game: open the panel and choose Credits.

Contact

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/andreseloysv/zoo_public/issues (RelaGame). Please mention your device model and the system version.
