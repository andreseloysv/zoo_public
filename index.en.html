The zoo starts talking when you stop walking

Your Zoo is an audio guide for Frankfurt Zoo. It works out which enclosure you are facing and tells that animal's story — and it would rather stay quiet than name the wrong animal.

The whole zoo ships inside the app: 31 enclosures, 77 species with a spoken story and five rotating facts each, images and the path geometry. No account, no sign-in, no analytics. You need no signal in the park — GPS works via satellite.

Three screens

Karte — map

Where you are

The enclosures from OpenStreetMap, your 3D explorer turning with the compass, and a “near you” sheet showing what is playing.

Tier-Story — guide

What you hear

The animal's story, a “super fact” that changes on every visit, and a player with speed control and a read-along transcript.

Your Zoo — pass

Your card

Photograph your membership card once. The app reads the QR code, the name and the expiry, and shows it at the turnstile — even without a signal.

What the app does — and what it does not

  • Offline: every text, image, piece of map geometry and the speech itself live on the device. The network is used only for content updates — and for the Apple base map under the enclosures.
  • Automatic: location and compass decide which enclosure is meant. When that is not clear enough, the app stays silent. Why it stays quiet.
  • Three languages: German, English, Spanish — interface, texts and speech.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers. Nothing about you leaves the device.
  • Not in this version: recorded narration (stories are read out by the device), zoos other than Frankfurt, an offline base map.
